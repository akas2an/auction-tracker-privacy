Privacy Policy

eBay Auction Tracker

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. About this application

eBay Auction Tracker is an independent application designed to help users monitor items in their eBay Watchlist. It is not operated by eBay.

2. Information we process

When you connect an eBay account, the application may process your eBay user identifier, authorized Watchlist item information, and OAuth credentials.

It may also store preferences such as hidden items, budget limits, and notification settings.

3. How information is used

Information is used to connect your eBay account, display watched items, synchronize auction information, and support your selected application features.

4. Storage and security

Application data is intended to be stored using server-side infrastructure provided by Netlify. Access credentials must not be exposed in public client-side code.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers as needed to operate the service. eBay account authorization is handled through eBay's own sign-in service.

6. Retention and deletion

Account-linked information is retained only as needed for the application's operation. When a verified eBay marketplace account deletion notification is received, associated stored account data is deleted.

7. Your choices

You may revoke the application's access through your eBay account settings. Revoking access does not necessarily delete data already stored by the application.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact: aka.s2an@gmail.com

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when application features or data practices change.
